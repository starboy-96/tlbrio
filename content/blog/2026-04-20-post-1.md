## The Problem of Off-Brand Slides
Many companies struggle with maintaining a consistent brand image across all their presentations. This is often due to a lack of standardised templates and guidelines, leading to a multitude of off-brand slides being created. While it may seem like a minor issue, the truth is that off-brand slides can have a significant impact on a company's productivity and overall brand reputation.

## The Time-Consuming Task of Reformatting
When slides are not formatted consistently, it can be a time-consuming task to reformat them. This can involve changing fonts, colours, and layouts to match the company's brand guidelines. The time spent on reformatting could be better spent on more important tasks, such as creating engaging content or rehearsing presentations. 
* A survey found that employees spend an average of 2 hours per week reformatting slides, which translates to around 100 hours per year.
* This is equivalent to around 2-3 weeks of full-time work per year, which could be better spent on core tasks.
* Furthermore, reformatting slides can be a frustrating task, leading to decreased job satisfaction and increased stress levels.

## The Impact on Brand Consistency
Off-brand slides can also have a negative impact on a company's brand consistency. When presentations are not formatted consistently, it can give the impression that the company is disorganised or lacking attention to detail. This can be particularly damaging for companies in professional services, finance, sales, marketing, and HR, where a professional image is crucial.
* A consistent brand image is essential for building trust and credibility with clients and customers.
* Off-brand slides can undermine this effort, making it harder to establish a strong brand reputation.
* Moreover, inconsistent branding can also make it harder to communicate the company's message and values.

## The Solution: Standardised Templates and Guidelines
So, how can companies avoid the hidden cost of off-brand slides? The answer lies in implementing standardised templates and guidelines. By providing employees with a set of pre-approved templates and guidelines, companies can ensure that all presentations are formatted consistently and in line with the company's brand image.
* Standardised templates can be created for different types of presentations, such as sales pitches, marketing materials, and training sessions.
* Guidelines can be established for fonts, colours, layouts, and imagery to ensure consistency across all presentations.
* Additionally, companies can also provide training and support to help employees understand the importance of brand consistency and how to use the templates and guidelines effectively.

## Implementing a Solution
Implementing a solution to the problem of off-brand slides requires a proactive approach. Companies need to take the time to develop standardised templates and guidelines, and provide training and support to employees. This may require an initial investment of time and resources, but it can pay off in the long run by saving time and improving brand consistency.
* Companies can start by conducting an audit of their current presentation materials to identify areas for improvement.
* They can then develop a set of standardised templates and guidelines that reflect the company's brand image and values.
* Finally, they can provide training and support to employees to help them understand the importance of brand consistency and how to use the templates and guidelines effectively.

## Taking Control of Your Presentations
By taking control of your presentations and implementing standardised templates and guidelines, you can avoid the hidden cost of off-brand slides and improve your company's brand consistency. This can help to build trust and credibility with clients and customers, and establish a strong brand reputation. 
The key takeaway is to be proactive in developing a solution to the problem of off-brand slides, and to provide employees with the tools and support they need to create high-quality, on-brand presentations. By doing so, you can save time, improve productivity, and enhance your company's overall brand image.
